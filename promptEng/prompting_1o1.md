# what can GenAI be used for?
Help you with the work that you do, but not do it for you.

# what is prompting?
- prompting is the use of instructions to  genAI model to generate a desired output.
- it is a way to communicate with an ai model and guide it to produce the desired output.
- to get the best results with ai, you must define the prompts precisely and clearly to ensure that the ai model understands what you want it to do. bad prompt results in bad output, and vise versa.

# prompt framework 
- Task: describe the task that you wnat the ai to help you with.
    - persona: what expertise you want the ai to have when performing the task.
    - format: the format of the output you want to receive from the ai.
- Context: the information that the ai needs to know to perform the task accurately and precisely.
- References (could have): any additional information that you can provide the ai in terms of examples or options.
- Evaluate: analyze if the input gave you the output that you desired, and if not, identify what went wrong and how to fix it: either with more context or greater task detail.
- Iterate: repeat the process until you get the desired output.
Order of the is less important than the content itself.

# Iteration methods
If you are not getting teh desired output, always iterate and try again. you can follow one of the following methods:
- Revisit the prompting framework and identify what is missing or what can be improved.
- Seperate you prompt into shorter and concise sentences/prompts.
- Try a different phrasing or switching to an analogous task.

# Modalities 
Modalities are the different formats that a GenAI tool can recieve or produce information.
